# AI Disclosure

This project uses AI/ML components for the following purposes:

## Task 1 – Service Lateness Prediction
Predicts the probability that a logistics service will be late based on historical patterns.

## Task 2 – Demand Forecasting
Forecasts future logistics demand to support resource planning.

## Responsible AI
- Models are trained on anonymised data.
- Predictions are advisory; final decisions remain with human operators.
- Regular bias audits are conducted.
